Active-AFK Combat

You auto-attack nearby foes. Tap the right zone to strike faster — each tap unleashes a quick swing. Drag the left zone to move.

The No-Loss Loop

You cannot die. When overwhelmed, your Vigor drains and progress slows. Re-engage to restore it. Stand still, and you idle-farm at half speed.

Scaling Loot

Every drop scales to your level. Hunt for better Rarity (Common→Rare→Epic) and Affixes, not bigger numbers.

The Abyss Deepens

Slay the Warden in each chamber to descend. Each depth changes the chamber's nature.
