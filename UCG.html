About the United Chess Guild

Our Philosophy

At the United Chess Guild, we believe that Elo rating — while a useful and important tool for matchmaking — is ultimately a volatile, transient number. It rises and falls with every game, swayed by streaks, opponents, and circumstance. It does not tell you how hard someone has worked, how many battles they have fought, or how deep their commitment to the game truly runs.

What we respect is the grind. The accumulated body of games played, studied, and won. Win volume is a measure of dedication that cannot be taken away by a bad weekend. We call this your Verified Win Count, and it is the only number that matters here.

What the UCG Does

The UCG is a registry and community platform for chess players who want to be recognized for their persistence above all else. Members create a profile, self-report their verified win count, and link their external platform profiles — Chess.com, Lichess, or FIDE — so that the community can confirm the numbers. There is no automated API scraping; verification is built on transparency and trust.

Members can update their win count at any time as they continue to accumulate games. The leaderboard reflects the current standings in real time.

Variants

The UCG recognizes all time controls and rule variants as legitimate chess. Whether you grind Classical, Blitz, Bullet, Ultrabullet, Crazyhouse, 3-Check, or any other format — every win counts toward your total. Wins are wins. Your preferred variant is displayed on your profile and in the standings as a point of identity, not hierarchy.

Title System

The UCG title system is inspired by the trades — a recognition of experience, output, and mastery earned through volume of work rather than peak performance.

Apprentice
0 – 999 wins. No prefix. You are learning the grind. Every win is a brick laid.
JM Journeyman
1,000+ wins. You have proven staying power. The prefix JM appears before your name on the leaderboard and your profile.
M Master
5,000+ wins. You are the craft. The prefix M appears before your name. Few reach this mark. Those who do have earned permanent respect in this registry.

The Workshop

The Workshop is the UCG's community forum. Log in to contribute — share your insights on openings, your journey toward a milestone, or anything else the grind has taught you.

Impersonation & Account Abuse

Because verification links are self-reported, it is possible for a bad actor to add someone else's Chess.com, Lichess, or FIDE profile to their UCG account in an attempt to impersonate them or falsely claim their win record. We take this seriously.

If you believe someone has linked your external profile without your permission, or is otherwise impersonating you on the UCG platform, please contact us immediately at [email protected]. Include your UCG username (if you have one), the offending account name, and a link to the external profile being misused. We will investigate and remove the fraudulent account.
